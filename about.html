Treasurer: building the program around the satellite

A club that flies balloons can run on a shared spreadsheet and a few emails. A club that launches a satellite needs reliable funding records, a launch provider, and a legal entity that can sign contracts. When I took over the books, the finances were spread across old spreadsheets with missing transactions and no clear balance for each funding source. Since then I've rebuilt that system, kept funding coming in, opened launch talks for KLEO, and helped set up the corporation those talks require.

A purchasing and budget system the whole club uses

I audited the old records and moved the budget from scattered Excel workbooks to Google Sheets with two AppSheet apps. Project leads submit bill-of-materials requests in one app. I record each purchase against the funding source that pays for it in the other. The final price and status flow back to the team's BOM, and balances update automatically.

Before rollout I tested the full request-to-purchase loop, including confirming that balances returned to their original values after a test purchase was removed. I also wrote a user guide and handover notes so the next treasurer can run it without me.

Finding KLEO a ride to orbit

Launch slots for a single university CubeSat are scarce and expensive, and most rideshare providers aren't set up to deal with a student club. After SmallSat 2026 I started reaching out to launch providers directly. I'm now in active talks with four of them.

That means working through each provider's integration requirements, schedule and cost, and turning their answers into what the KLEO team needs to have ready. Those talks also exposed a problem: as a student organization, the club couldn't sign the NDAs and contracts involved. That's what led us to incorporate.

Incorporation

I helped incorporate Knights Satellite Club Inc., a Florida not-for-profit that lets the club sign the external agreements KLEO needs. I prepared and filed documents, coordinated the founding directors, obtained the EIN and completed the tax forms. I serve as a founding director and the corporation's Treasurer, and I'm authorized to handle its routine NDAs.
